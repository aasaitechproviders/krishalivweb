About KRISHA LIV

A more thoughtful way to choose fresh food.

We are building a food experience that connects produce, personal preferences and nutrition information in one clear journey.

Our story

Make the daily decision easier.

Food choices become difficult when time, household preferences and changing needs all meet at once. KRISHA LIV, a brand of FusionMAC Impex, is being designed to bring fresh produce and curated baskets together with a profile-led digital experience.

Customers will be able to share their preferences, food restrictions and wellness goals, explore suggested products and basket options, review available nutrient information, and choose a delivery rhythm that suits their routine. Our aim is to make each recommendation explainable, practical and open to adjustment.

Our approach

Personal choices, grounded in food information

The planned recommendation system combines declared preferences and restrictions with product-level nutrition data and clear review points.

1

Start with a profile

Household information, preferences, restrictions and goals shape the choices shown.

2

Explain the suggestion

Food options should show relevant nutrients, quantities and practical context, using validated data and transparent methods.

3

Review with people

Nutritionists can review the approach and support individual cases where automated food guidance is insufficient.

“Science-backed” means a commitment to validated nutrition sources, documented recommendation rules and nutritionist review before health-related features launch. No score alone can diagnose or treat a condition.

What guides us

Vision, mission and goals

Vision

Fresh and varied food choices that more households can make with confidence.

Mission

Connect quality food selection, understandable nutrition information and convenient basket delivery around real household needs.

Goals

Support practical eating routines, improve transparency around product choices and invite continuous customer feedback.
